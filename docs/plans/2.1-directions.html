Visual directions for the app shell (ADR 0010)

Each direction in light and dark, on a phone-sized deck list with a sample card. Only the colours, type and corners change between directions. Layout and navigation are the same in all three. Card text in a real card comes from the note type's own CSS inside the sandboxed card frame. The built-in note types would use the direction's font family.

A. Paper (recommended)

A study notebook: warm paper and ink, one terracotta accent, serif headings and card text, system sans for controls, flat surfaces with a thin border instead of shadows.

B. Calm blue

Cool neutrals, one blue accent, system fonts throughout, softer corners and a light shadow. Clean and familiar. The safest choice, and the most likely to read as generic.

C. High contrast

Black and white, blue accent in light and yellow in dark, larger base type, square corners. The fastest to scan and the strongest on accessibility. Also the most austere.
